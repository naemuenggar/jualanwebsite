## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary visitor (who the rendered page must convince):** an Indonesian UMKM / small-to-medium business decision-maker — a cafe owner, clinic manager, shop owner, small founder — evaluating whether to commission a website. Often non-technical, budget-conscious, browsing on a mid-range phone over a variable connection, wary of "ribet" (complicated) tech vendors.

**Actual audience (who the showcase must impress):** the owner's **prospective clients** browsing the owner's portfolio site. They are judging build quality and credibility — "can this person deliver a real, professional website for me?" The whole piece exists to make that answer feel like an obvious yes.

**Operator (who could rebrand it):** someone in the Indonesian UMKM/web-services context who swaps in their own brand, services, prices, proof, and contact. "Webkriya" is a placeholder identity, not a committed brand.

## Product Purpose

A **showcase / demo landing page** that lives on the owner's portfolio site as proof of the caliber of website they can build. Its real audience is the owner's **prospective clients**, who must look at it and think "this is a genuine, professional, premium product" — not an AI one-shot. The demo subject is an Indonesian web-services offering for UMKM ("Webkriya — jasa pembuatan website untuk UMKM"), and the piece doubles as a rebrandable template (opinionated section flow + hierarchy other content can drop into). Success = a viewer on the portfolio reads it as the real deal; the depicted UMKM visitor would feel reassured enough to reach out.

**Origin / anti-reference:** the current implementation was generated by a friend in a single AI one-shot. Treat its execution as evidence and anti-reference — the goal is to remove every "AI-generated" tell (fabricated domains, generic stock, uniform spacing, flat depth, templated defaults) and lift the craft until it reads as authored, real work.

## Positioning

Indonesian-UMKM-native by construction, not a translated generic template: bahasa Indonesia copy, Rupiah ("jt") pricing, WhatsApp-led contact, and locally recognizable sectors (kedai kopi, klinik, toko fashion, bengkel, coworking). The voice is deliberately casual and low-anxiety ("tanpa ribet", "ngobrol santai", "konsultasi gratis") to disarm non-technical buyers. The reusable value is the opinionated-but-swappable structure — a neighboring template competes on the same sections but rarely on this locale-native tone and flow together.

## Operating Context

- **Channel reality:** WhatsApp is the dominant contact/lead channel; primary CTAs deep-link to `wa.me`. Section navigation is hash-anchored (`#layanan`, `#portofolio`, `#proses`, `#harga`, `#testimoni`, `#kontak`).
- **Trust path:** portfolio / work samples are the primary credibility mechanism — the visitor is expected to look at the work before committing to contact.
- **Section flow (demo):** hero → trust marquee → services grid → portfolio bento → process steps → why-us → pricing tiers → testimonials → CTA → footer.
- **Pricing convention:** Rupiah in "jt" (juta) shorthand, tiered Starter / Business / Enterprise, with "Custom" for open-scope work.
- **Device context:** mobile-first; must stay legible and tappable on small, lower-end devices.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Content is data-driven:** all marketing content lives in `lib/data.ts` (services, projects, process, why-us, pricing tiers, testimonials, stats, trust categories, nav, contact). This file is the single swap point for rebranding.
- **Static marketing surface:** no backend, no real forms, no auth. CTAs resolve to a WhatsApp deep link and in-page section anchors.
- **Stack** (already fixed by the codebase): Next.js App Router (v16) + React 19, Tailwind CSS 3 with custom tokens (`ink`, `mist`, `hair`, `zap`, etc.), `framer-motion` for reveal animation, `lucide-react` icons. Fonts: Inter (`--font-inter`), Space Grotesk (`--font-space-grotesk`, display), Space Mono (`--font-space-mono`).
- **Language:** all copy is Indonesian; `<html lang="id">`, `locale: id_ID`. This is a binding constraint, not incidental.
- **Images:** portfolio/hero previews currently pull from external Unsplash URLs — placeholders. Because this is a showcase, imagery must read as credible real work, not generic stock; avoid images that obviously read as stock filler.
- **No fabricated domains (hard constraint):** the current fake domains (`kopisenja.id`, `mitrasehat.co.id`, `noirfashion.id`, `webkriya.id`, etc.) are AI one-shot tells and must be removed. Do not invent replacement domains. Present portfolio/mockups without domain-name claims (neutral device/browser chrome, project name + sector only, or no URL bar).

## Brand Commitments

- **"Webkriya" is a placeholder persona, NOT a committed brand.** Name, logo treatment, contact details, and domain (`webkriya.id`) are demo defaults meant to be swapped by the operator.
- **Binding constraints to preserve across any redesign:**
  - Indonesian language throughout.
  - Casual, jargon-light, reassuring voice — reduce the perceived complexity/risk of getting a website; never talk down, never over-formal.
  - Rupiah pricing in "jt" shorthand.
  - WhatsApp-first contact pattern.
  - UMKM sector framing (local small-business categories).

## Product Principles

1. **Indonesian-UMKM-native by default** — bahasa, Rupiah, WhatsApp, and local sectors are the baseline, never a localization layer bolted onto a generic template.
2. **Reassure over impress** — plain-language, "tanpa ribet" tone that lowers the perceived risk and complexity of commissioning a website for a non-technical buyer.
3. **Portfolio-forward trust** — let the work carry credibility; surface samples prominently before asking the visitor to make contact.
4. **Swappable content, opinionated structure** — everything specific (brand, prices, proof, contact) is designed to be replaced; the reusable value is the section flow, hierarchy, and tone.
5. **No fabricated proof** — sample stats and testimonials stay visibly generic and are never dressed up as verified claims of a real track record.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Audience is broad, non-technical Indonesian UMKM owners on mobile, often low-end devices and variable connections. Requirements that follow from that: mobile-first and tap-friendly targets, plain readable Indonesian (no unexplained jargon), sufficient contrast and legible type at small sizes, and content that does not depend on fast connections or heavy assets to make sense.
